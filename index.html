Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Virethalyn (“the App,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application. We built Virethalyn to be private by design: we do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Virethalyn does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or mailing address
  • Your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics
  • Crash reports, logs, or behavioral data

2. Local Data Storage

All data you enter into Virethalyn stays on your device. Information is stored locally using your device’s secure storage mechanisms and is never uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate any backend servers or cloud databases for this App.

If you uninstall Virethalyn, all locally stored data is deleted along with the App, unless you have manually exported it.

3. No Third-Party Sharing

Since we do not collect any personal information, we have no data to sell, rent, or share with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or any third parties.

4. Permissions

Virethalyn requests access only to device features that are strictly necessary for core functionality. We do not request permissions unrelated to the App’s purpose, and we never access data in the background without your direct action.

5. Children’s Privacy

Virethalyn is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Security

Because all data remains on your device, its security depends on your device’s built-in protections (such as passcodes, biometrics, and OS-level encryption). We recommend keeping your device updated and using strong device security settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of Virethalyn after changes means you accept the updated policy.
